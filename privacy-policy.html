1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lilac Labs NGT LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information about you when you visit our website, contact us, subscribe to our communications, or use our products and services (collectively, the “Services”).

This policy covers two broad audiences: (1) visitors to our website and people who interact with our company — such as prospective customers, newsletter subscribers, and business contacts; and (2) users of our Sunny AI Tutor product. Because Sunny is directed to children, a dedicated section governs children’s information and our practices under the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”). See Section 9.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

We do not currently use cookies for analytics or for cross-context behavioral advertising. If we add analytics or advertising technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to describe them — and, where required by law, provide a consent mechanism — before they take effect.

9Children’s Privacy — Sunny AI Tutor (COPPA)

Our company website is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our marketing website, contact forms, or newsletter.

Our Sunny AI Tutor product, however, is designed for children in grades K–5 (approximately ages 5 to 11). We knowingly collect personal information from children within Sunny and comply with COPPA. The following practices apply specifically to Sunny:

  • Parent-managed accounts: Accounts are created and managed by a parent or legal guardian (“Parent”). A child may use Sunny only under a Parent’s account.
  • Verifiable parental consent: We obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child, and we provide Parents direct notice of our practices before collection.
  • What Sunny collects: Within Sunny we may collect a child’s first name or nickname, grade level, learning activity and progress, and — where voice features are enabled — voice recordings used to provide speech-based tutoring. Voice data is treated as sensitive.
  • How it is used: Children’s information is used to deliver and personalize tutoring, generate feedback, maintain safety, and improve the product. We process this information ourselves using secure cloud infrastructure that operates on our behalf under a data processing agreement, with appropriate security measures and restrictions on access to and use of the data. We do not sell children’s personal information or share it with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No selling, no ad targeting: We do not sell children’s personal information and do not use it for targeted or behavioral advertising.
  • Parental rights: Parents can review and manage their child’s information, refuse further collection, and request deletion directly from the parent dashboard while logged in to their account. Because account access verifies your identity, no separate verification is typically required. For requests that cannot be completed through the dashboard — such as full account deletion or withdrawing consent — or if you are unable to access your account, contact us using the information in Section 2.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information without the required parental consent, contact us using the details in Section 2. We will take reasonable steps to verify the request and the requester’s relationship to the child, and will promptly delete the information if verification confirms consent was not properly obtained.
