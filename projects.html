01 / Why Sunny

Fluency is not
teaching.

AI can explain anything, and be wrong about it with exactly the same confidence. For an adult, that's an inconvenience. For a seven-year-old learning subtraction for the first time, a wrong answer delivered warmly is worse than no answer at all.

02 / How Sunny works

Layers,
not prompts.

Sunny treats the AI as one part of a larger system, not the whole thing. Every lesson passes through a series of deliberate steps before a student ever sees it.

  1. 01

    Curriculum

    A structured map of what students should learn, grade by grade, aligned to published educational standards. Sunny chooses the next skill; the AI never decides what school looks like.

  2. 02

    Correctness

    The answer to every problem, and the steps to get there, are worked out before the AI is asked for a single word. Accuracy isn't something we hope for; it's settled before the lesson is written.

  3. 03

    Verification

    Everything the AI writes is reviewed against the standards for that student's grade before it's shown. Anything that falls short is sent back with the reason, and redone until it passes.

  4. 04

    Grounding

    Lessons about the real world are built from vetted source material rather than the AI's memory, so a student exploring a favorite subject gets something new, and true, every time.

  5. 05

    Safety

    Anything a student types is screened before a lesson is written, with the bar set for their age. Safety comes before the writing, not after it.

  6. 06

    Mastery that sticks

    Sunny measures mastery by what a student keeps, not by a lucky streak of right answers, and brings skills back for practice right when a little review does the most good. Knowing something on Friday isn't the same as still knowing it in March.

03 / Built to last

It gets better
as AI does.

Sunny isn't built around any one AI. It already teaches reading and math with different AI behind the scenes, all held to the same standards, so when better AI arrives it's an upgrade for students, not a fresh start for us.

The curriculum, the safety standards, the checking, and everything we've learned about how students actually learn are the parts we built ourselves, and they're the parts that last. Every time AI gets better, students get lessons explained a little more clearly. Nothing starts over.

Better AI comes in. The teaching stays.

  1. 01

    Built to welcome better AI

    Sunny isn't tied to any one AI. When a better one comes along, it slots right in, and everything students rely on stays exactly where it was.

  2. 02

    Better, never just different

    Before any new AI gets to teach a student, it has to meet the same standards as the one it's replacing. No surprises, no backsliding.

    The parts that last

    The curriculum, the checking, and the safety are ours, and they keep getting better year after year, no matter what AI is inside.
