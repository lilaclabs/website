1The Short Version

Just Looking Photos (“Just Looking”) doesn’t collect, store, or share any personal information. Your photos stay on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and we never see what’s on your phone.

2Your Photos and Videos

With your permission, Just Looking reads your photo library only to show it on your device. It never uploads, shares, changes, or deletes anything.

If you use iCloud Photos and a photo’s original is kept in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple so it can be shown. That happens between your device and Apple, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The Places tab shows your photos on Apple Maps satellite imagery, placed using the locations stored with them. Apple receives the map areas you look at, as with any map app. Your photos and their locations are never sent.

3The On-Device Nudity Check

If you turn it on, Just Looking checks photos and videos with Apple’s Sensitive Content Analysis, which runs entirely on your device. The results (which photos are fine to show) are saved only on your device, left out of backups, and never sent to us or anyone else. Checking can continue while your device is locked and charging; it still happens only on your device.

4Apple Music

If you connect Apple Music, Just Looking uses Apple’s MusicKit to list, search, and play playlists, and to look up whether songs are explicit so they can be skipped. These requests go to Apple, under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we receive nothing. Song ratings are remembered on your device so explicit songs can still be skipped offline.

5Your PIN and Face ID

The owner PIN is stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain, as a one-way hash rather than the PIN itself. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app is only told whether it succeeded. Your settings are also stored only on your device.

6Showing Photos on a TV

When you use Screen Mirroring, Just Looking shows photos on your TV through iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

7Crash Reports and Feedback

If you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your device’s settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple provides that information to us. It can include your device model, iOS version, crash logs, and any comments or screenshots you choose to send. We use it only to fix problems and improve the app.

8Children

Just Looking doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9Sharing and Selling

We don’t sell or share any information about you, because we don’t collect any.

10Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
